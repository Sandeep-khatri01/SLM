What you're looking at

Two decoder-only transformers, about 19M parameters each, trained from random initialization on 48 curated debate topics. No pretrained weights, no distillation, no fine-tuning. One model only ever saw positive keyword lists; the other only negative ones.

For the 48 known topics, keywords come directly from the generative models. For any other topic, a TF-IDF retrieval layer finds the nearest known topics and returns their keywords — honestly labeled as a retrieval fallback.

The retrieval layer is a bridge to true open-domain behavior: it handles arbitrary input gracefully while being transparent about what it knows and doesn't know.
